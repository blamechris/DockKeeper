## Extracted facts

**Editions.** Lite (App Store, free + IAP: $1.99/mo, $16.99/yr, $39.99 full unlock; 7-day full trial) · Plus (App Store, $39.99 lifetime) · Pro (website-only, "upcoming", pricing unstated). Developer: Ihor July. All list macOS 10.9+.

**Lite features (vendor-listed).** Lock Dock to preferred display; temporary move via key combo; hide during screen sharing/meetings; menu-bar toggle; auto-launch (macOS 12+); paid: hotkey control, hidden icons.

**Plus adds (vendor-listed).** "Dock Follows Mouse", "Dock Follows Active Window"/apps, Apple Shortcuts + URL scheme automation, official Raycast extension, CLI, hotkey customization, Sidecar/DisplayLink support, position restoration after sleep.

**Pro claims (vendor-listed, unreleased).** "Place Dock on any screen edge", "move vertical Dock across displays", "works with disabled Spaces settings", "unlock free space around the Dock for windows".

**Stated limitations (App Store listings, both Lite and Plus).**
- Requires **minimum two connected displays** to function.
- Works **only with Dock positioned at bottom**.
- **"Displays have separate Spaces" must be ENABLED.**
- Command+Tab remains tied to the Dock's location.

**Permissions/privacy (vendor-stated).** Requires **Accessibility** access; fully sandboxed on the App Store; "operates entirely offline", no data collection claimed.

**Reception note (App Store, low sample).** Positive on function; "subscription pop-up is annoying" (Lite).

## E-5: Black-box observation — installed copy (2026-07-23)

DockLock Lite v1.4.8 (build 594) is legitimately installed on the dev machine
(not running during observations). Local preferences (`pro.docklock.lite`)
show: `settings.dock.control_mode = 1`, `lock_position = 1`, a
`workspaces` dictionary keyed by opaque display/workspace hashes with encoded
per-workspace rules, `jump.modifier` (the temporary-move hotkey), and
`ui.warn_incompatible_display = 1` — the product has a built-in
**"incompatible display" warning**, corroborating that its bottom-only,
summon-based approach does not work on all topologies (e.g. the stacked
portrait-above-laptop arrangement on this rig, where the bottom-edge summon
gesture demonstrably fails — see `docs/spikes/separate-spaces-pinning.md`).
